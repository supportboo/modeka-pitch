    FAQ

    Still have
    a question?

    How do I know which size to choose?
    Every model includes its size chart with chest, waist and sleeve measurements. If you're between two, write to us with your height and weight and we'll guide you.
    Are the touring jackets waterproof?
    Touring models feature a technical membrane and sealed seams. The level varies by range and is stated on each product page.
    Do they come with certified protectors?
    Yes. Protected models integrate CE-certified pieces at shoulders and elbows, and are usually ready for a back protector.
    Can one jacket work all year round?
    The 3-in-1 jackets combine shell, membrane and thermal liner: add or remove layers with the weather, so you wear them through all four seasons.
    Is there a women's specific cut?
    Many bestsellers have a women's version with its own cut, like the Panamericana II Lady. Ask us about the model you're interested in.
