Production

How it's made

Storyboard

The 25 frames in this bible are generated sketches (Nano Banana 2). They validate framing, light and action before video.

Video engine

Seedance 2.0 for face shots; Kling 3 Omni for B-roll and macro. Start/end frame per shot.

Voice + sound

VO ElevenLabs v3 + sound design (engine, rain, leather) and music per ad. Mixed in the master.

Formats

9:16 social + 16:9 TV. 18 s duration. Same material, two crops.

Continuity (hard rule): same face, same hairstyle and same clothing in ALL shots of an ad — hair never jumps from tied-up to loose between scenes. Every shot starts from the same model reference frame. Micro-expressions and eye contact are reserved for the close. No ad starts and ends in the same scene.
Creative preproduction · Boomatik for Modeka. Storyboard and prompts prior to video generation. Garments reproduced without altering the design; motorcycles and backgrounds illustrative.
